Everyday elements

Links, buttons, messages and form fields: the small things every page needs that the page mockups haven't decided yet. Each section shows a few versions side by side.

Links

Each card shows an ordinary link inside text, a standalone link, and the quieter kind ("Report a concern", "Back to top"; the same in every version). Underneath, the four states side by side. The keyboard-focus ring is the same in every version: it shows where you are when moving with the Tab key.

Buttons

Four kinds of button: the main one (one per area, the most important action), ordinary ones, quiet ones that look like links, and danger for removing things. Each card is one family of colours, shown first in real situations, then every kind in every state. "Not available" stays readable grey rather than see-through. Danger buttons are outlined or softly filled, so they are clear without shouting; solid red is kept for the final "Yes, delete" (see the next section).

Confirming a delete

Before something is removed, the site asks once more. Today the app uses the browser's own pop-up: small grey text, a heading like "localhost says", and buttons labelled only "OK" and "Cancel". Two alternatives, in the Dark family. Press "Delete this work" in B to see it work.

Messages

The short messages the site shows after something happens. Four kinds, each with its own calm colour and icon: success, information, warning and error. Every message starts with a few words in bold that say what happened, and has a "Close" in words. Today they float small in the top-right corner of the screen. Here we decide only how they look; where they appear is decided later in the real app.

Try it

A small page that scrolls inside its frame. It starts scrolled down to the bottom of a form, as if you had just filled it in. Press a button to see a message, with the look and place chosen above the frame. Only a first impression: the real decision on placement is made in the app.

Form fields

Text boxes, dropdowns and checkboxes, as on the vendor pages. The same in every version: the label always above the field; help text between the label and the field, so it is read before typing; "(optional)" in words rather than a *; 16px text inside fields; edges dark enough to find; a mistake shown with a red edge and words under the field; larger checkboxes than today. The versions differ only in the shape of the field box. You can type in them and press Tab to move between them.
