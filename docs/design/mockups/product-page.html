About this work

A crow I meet most mornings near Ross Bay, standing on the driftwood the way it always does — as if it owns the beach. The stones around the edge are from the same beach, painted one by one from ones I brought home.

The round canvas is meant to feel like looking through a porthole or into a tide pool.

Details

Painted in 2025. The sides are painted grey, so it can hang without a frame. Comes with a wire on the back, ready to hang.

Pickup and delivery

Pickup at the artist's studio in Fernwood. You arrange the time together in your messages after you request to buy.

Returns
