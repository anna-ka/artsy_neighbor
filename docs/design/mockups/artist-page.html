About the artist

I have painted in Victoria for over thirty years. Most mornings I walk the shoreline from Clover Point to Ross Bay, and the crows, otters and seals I meet there end up on my canvases. I work mostly in oils on round canvases.

Delivery options

Pick-up — from my studio in Fernwood; we arrange a time by message.
