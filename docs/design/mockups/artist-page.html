About the artist

I have painted in Victoria for over thirty years. Most mornings I walk the shoreline from Clover Point to Ross Bay, and the crows, otters and seals I meet there end up on my canvases. I work mostly in oils on round canvases — the circle feels like a window, or a tide pool.I grew up on a farm in the Okanagan and came to the coast to study at art school. After many years of teaching painting classes in community centres around the city, I now paint full time in a small studio in Fernwood, in the back of an old house with a big north-facing window. After many years of teaching painting classes in community centres around the city, I now paint full time in a small studio in Fernwood, in the back of an old house with a big north-facing window.

Every painting here is an original. I am always happy to talk about how a piece was made, to show work in progress at the studio, or to discuss a commission of your favourite place on the coast. Drop me a message — I usually reply the same day.
